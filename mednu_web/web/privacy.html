Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MedNU Healthcare Services Private Limited ("MedNU", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use:

  • MedNU: our app for patients (iOS and Android)
  • MedNU Service: our app for doctors and healthcare providers (iOS and Android)
  • our website mednu.in (together, the "Services")

We process personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and other applicable laws of India. By using the Services, you agree to this Policy.

Want to delete your account? You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing admin@mednu.in. See Section 9.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

WhoData
All usersName, mobile number, email address (optional), profile photo (optional), preferred language.
Patients (MedNU)Age or date of birth, gender, address for deliveries, symptoms and reasons for consultation, medical history, prescriptions, lab reports and other health documents you upload or receive, appointment and order history.
Doctors / providers (MedNU Service)Professional details such as qualifications, specialisation, medical registration number, clinic details, availability, identity and verification documents, and payout details (bank account or UPI) for receiving consultation fees.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, to show nearby doctors, clinics, pharmacies and labs, and for deliveries.
  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app version, crash logs, and a notification token so we can send you push notifications.
  • Camera, microphone and photos: only when you use them, for example during a video consultation or when you upload a document or photo.

c) Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment partner Razorpay. We do not collect or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN. We receive only a transaction reference, amount and payment status.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your phone number by OTP.
  • To let patients find, book and consult doctors, including secure video consultations.
  • To process orders for medicines and lab tests and arrange deliveries.
  • To share the necessary booking and health details between a patient and the doctor they choose to consult.
  • To verify doctors' credentials before they can offer consultations.
  • To process payments, refunds and doctor payouts.
  • To send appointment, order and account notifications by push notification, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To provide customer support, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep the Services secure.
  • To improve the Services and fix problems.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your health information for advertising.

3. Health information

Health information is sensitive personal data. We collect it only to provide healthcare services you request, and we share it only with the doctor, pharmacy or lab you choose, or as required by law. Doctors on MedNU are independent professionals and are responsible for the medical advice they give and for keeping patient records as required by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines and other applicable rules.

4. How we share information

We share personal data only as described below:

  • Between patients and doctors: to make a consultation possible.
  • Pharmacies, labs and delivery partners: only what they need to fulfil your order.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract:
    • Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, push notifications)
    • Agora (real-time video and voice consultations)
    • Razorpay (payments and doctor payouts)
    • Google Maps (location and maps)
    • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform (appointment and order messages)
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or MedNU.
  • Business transfers: if MedNU is involved in a merger or acquisition, subject to this Policy.

Some of these providers may store or process data on servers outside India. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your data stays protected as required by Indian law.

5. Data security

We use reasonable security practices, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, authentication and secure cloud infrastructure, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a data breach as required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Services. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records longer to meet legal, tax, medical-record or dispute-resolution obligations. Records we keep for these reasons are stored securely and used only for those purposes.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • withdraw consent (this may limit what features you can use);
  • request erasure of your data and deletion of your account;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@mednu.in.

8. Permissions

You can turn off location, camera, microphone, photos and notifications at any time in your phone's settings. Some features, such as video consultations or nearby search, may not work without them.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your MedNU or MedNU Service account at any time:

  • In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm.
  • By email: write to admin@mednu.in from your registered email, or include your registered mobile number, with the subject "Delete my account".

When you delete your account, we remove your profile, login and personal data as described in Section 6. Pending appointments or orders will be cancelled, and doctors' unpaid earnings will be settled before closure.

10. Children

The Services are not intended for use by anyone under 18 on their own. A parent or legal guardian may create an account and book consultations for a child, and is responsible for providing consent on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect children's data without verifiable parental consent.

11. Medical emergencies

MedNU is not an emergency service. In a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the app.
